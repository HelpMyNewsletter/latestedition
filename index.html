PICK ONE MONEY JOB

Three ways to put the connections to work.

1. Sell something you already have.

Use: Beehiiv + your existing product information.

Ask AI to read three recent editions and list your live offers. Match one offer to a problem those editions discuss. Have it draft a short section with the real price, who it helps, and one purchase link.

The money: completed purchases. Measure orders and receipts separately from clicks.

Check: a live listing doesn't prove delivery works. Open the checkout and verify the buyer's download or access path before promotion.

2. Build a sponsor shortlist worth acting on.

Use: web research + your audience brief + a spreadsheet.

Ask for five businesses that fit a recurring reader need. Require a source for each match, a published business contact route, and a proposed placement tied to your actual editorial calendar.

The money: a paid placement if a sponsor agrees. Research is the preparation; a signed deal is a separate result.

Check: verify contacts and claims. Review outreach before sending it, and use your actual audience figures.

3. Package a task you can deliver repeatedly.

Use: your process notes + a document tool + your newsletter platform.

Choose a job you've already solved: a handoff checklist, a planning worksheet, or a focused setup service. Ask AI to turn the process into instructions another person can follow. Test those instructions with someone who didn't build them.

The money: a useful download or a defined service. Price it with delivery time, fees, and support in mind.

Check: “AI made it” isn't the benefit. Explain what the buyer will be able to do.
