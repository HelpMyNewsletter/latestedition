THE SAFE BUILD

Build the exit before you build the follow-up.

Inside Beehiiv, the mechanical setup is short. The thinking should happen first.

01

Create a new automation. Select Clicked a link as the trigger.

02

Choose the URL carefully. Use exact match for one fixed destination. Use starts with only when tracking or subscriber-specific parameters change the end of the URL.

03

Add a humane delay. Give the reader time to act before you assume they didn't.

04

Check the stop condition. Purchased? Downloaded? Booked? Referred? If the goal happened, route them out of the pitch and into the right experience.

05

Send one useful next step. Answer the likely question, offer the missing resource or remind them once. Then stop.

THE NON-CREEPY RULES

A click is evidence. It isn't permission to invent a person.

01. Don't treat one click as a permanent identity.

02. Don't infer sensitive facts the reader never volunteered.

03. Don't keep pitching after the goal has already happened.

04. Don't use starts with so broadly that unrelated clicks enter the same journey.

05. Don't automate a follow-up that wouldn't feel respectful if you sent it personally.

My rule: use behavior to reduce irrelevance, not to increase pressure.

THE CLICK-TRIGGER SPEC

Fill this out before you touch the automation builder.

Trigger URL: The exact link or carefully chosen URL prefix.

What the click probably means: Interest, not certainty.

Delay: Enough time for the reader to act without being chased.

Stop condition: The purchase, booking, download, referral or other completed goal.

Exclusions: People who should never enter or should exit immediately.

One useful follow-up: The next message that earns its place.

Success metric: The behavior that should improve if the automation helps.

Review date: When you'll inspect results and shut it down if it isn't serving readers.
