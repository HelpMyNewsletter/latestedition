Field Notes / July 28, 2026

Yes, I use AI. No, I am not hiding it.

Substack now lets readers scan eligible writing for the likelihood of AI assistance. Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, and LinkedIn are also adding labels, disclosures, or provenance signals.

That does not frighten me. Teaching publishers to use AI responsibly is part of my work. The real question is whether a human still supplied the direction, judgment, verification, and final approval.

Reader research reflects that distinction. People are far more comfortable when a human leads and AI assists than when AI is allowed to operate as the author.

The Transparency Shift

The platforms are making more of the production process visible.

Substack: Readers can request an AI-likelihood scan on eligible posts, Notes, replies, and comments longer than 100 words. The result is still an estimate. It can't prove who originated the idea, selected the evidence, or approved the finished work.

Meta and TikTok: Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and TikTok use creator disclosures, detected signals, and content credentials to label certain AI-generated or heavily altered media.

YouTube: Realistic synthetic or meaningfully altered content requires disclosure. Ordinary production assistance, including outlining, captions, titles, and idea development, generally does not.

Pinterest and LinkedIn: Labels and Content Credentials increasingly reveal how media was made, edited, or generated.

The useful distinction: A label can show that a tool participated. It can't measure originality, care, truthfulness, expertise, or responsibility.

MCP / Developer Mode

Connect ChatGPT to the systems where your editorial work actually lives.

Model Context Protocol gives ChatGPT controlled access to approved tools and data. Depending on the server, it can search an archive, retrieve analytics, read documents, create drafts, or update records.

01 / Enable Developer Mode

Open ChatGPT Settings → Security and login → Developer mode. In a managed workspace, an administrator may have to allow it first.

02 / Gather the Connection Details

Get the provider's public HTTPS MCP endpoint, authentication method, connection name, and description. Don't connect an untrusted server just because someone posted its URL.

03 / Add the MCP Server

Open ChatGPT Plugins, select the plus button, enter the server URL, complete the details, and authorize the account when prompted.

04 / Inspect Every Tool

Search and read don't carry the same risk as publish, send, update, delete, or archive. Disable the tools you don't need.

05 / Run a Read-Only Test

“List the available tools and explain each one. Don't create, update, publish, send, archive, or delete anything.” Then retrieve records you already know and confirm the output.

06 / Test Writes in Draft

Use a sandbox, duplicate, or unpublished draft. Name the exact tool and require confirmation before anything publishes, sends, deletes, or changes live data.

MCP's wiring, not authorship. It gives AI access to the information and actions you authorize. You're still the one deciding what the work means and what happens next.

The Editorial Chain

Use AI to strengthen editorial control, not quietly replace it.

  • 01You set the premise. Decide what happened, why it matters, what you believe, and what the reader needs.
  • 02MCP retrieves the evidence. Pull from your archive, interviews, analytics, research, and project documents.
  • 03AI helps interrogate the material. Find contradictions, missing context, weak claims, and stronger structures.
  • 04AI helps with production. Draft, edit, code, format, repurpose, and prepare assets under your direction.
  • 05You sign off. Verify claims, restore your voice, inspect every link, and accept responsibility.
