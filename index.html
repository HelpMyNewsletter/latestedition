Quick action guide
What I would do right now
1. Do not rebuild what already works.
Before replacing a working template, website, automation, or subscriber journey, test first. Duplicate your assets. Check mobile. Test every link. Verify your tracking. New does not automatically mean better for your publication.
2. Pick one goal before choosing a feature.
Do not begin with, “Which new feature should I activate?”

Begin with, “What does my publication need most?”

More subscribers? Better engagement? More revenue? Stronger retention? Fewer tools?
3. Use Copilot as an analyst first.
Start by asking questions about your strongest content, most engaged readers, welcome sequence, subscriber segments, and conversion gaps.

Let it surface opportunities before you hand it control of campaigns or automations.
4. Build one useful asset.
Not six experiments.

Build one better landing page, offer funnel, welcome sequence, paid product, community pilot, sponsor page, podcast workflow, or re-engagement campaign.
5. Give yourself a 30-day rollout.
Week 1: Audit your current setup and software stack.
Week 2: Test the relevant new tools safely.
Week 3: Build one meaningful asset.
Week 4: Review the results and decide what deserves expansion.
Best bets by goal
Where I would focus first
If growth is the goal: Review your website, landing pages, signup forms, lead magnets, and conversion path.

If engagement is the goal: Explore Community, but only if you can give readers a clear reason to return between newsletters.

If monetization is the goal: Strengthen one revenue path first, such as paid subscriptions, sponsorships, digital products, memberships, services, or events.

If expansion is the goal: Consider podcasting only if audio fits your brand, audience, and production capacity.

If simplification is the goal: Audit the outside tools you are paying for and determine which ones Beehiiv may now be able to replace.
What can wait
Please do not do these this week
• Do not rebuild your entire website because the new builder looks interesting.
• Do not launch a community without a purpose and participation plan.
• Do not start a podcast solely because the feature now exists.
• Do not replace working funnels without testing the new version first.
• Do not allow AI to publish strategy you have not reviewed.
